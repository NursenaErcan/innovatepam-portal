# Phase 7 Scoring System - Submitter Guide

## Overview

After your idea has been fully reviewed and a **Final Decision** is made (accepted or rejected), you can view evaluation scores on your submitter dashboard. These scores represent the review panel's assessment of your idea across three key dimensions.

## When Can I See Scores?

You can see scores **only after**:
- ✅ Final Decision has been made (idea status is "Accepted" or "Rejected")
- ✅ You are viewing your own idea
- ✅ On submitter dashboard under "Your Ideas"

You **cannot** see scores:
- ❌ While idea is still in review (submitted, screening, technical review, etc.)
- ❌ For other people's ideas
- ❌ Until final decision made

## How to View Your Score Summary

1. **Log in** to your submitter account
2. **Go to Dashboard** (Your Ideas section)
3. **Find your idea** in the "Your Ideas" list
4. **Scroll down** to "Evaluation Scores" section (appears for accepted/rejected ideas only)
5. **See your scores** for Innovation, Feasibility, and Business Impact

## Understanding Your Scores

### The Three Dimensions

#### Innovation Score (1-5)
**What they assessed**: How novel, creative, and original is your idea?
- **1**: Limited originality or incremental improvement
- **2**: Some novel aspects but incremental
- **3**: Moderately innovative, new approach
- **4**: Highly innovative, significant novelty
- **5**: Breakthrough idea, highly original concept

#### Feasibility Score (1-5)
**What they assessed**: How realistic and achievable is your idea?
- **1**: Major technical or logistical barriers
- **2**: Significant implementation challenges
- **3**: Achievable with moderate effort/cost
- **4**: Well-aligned with capabilities, minor challenges
- **5**: Highly feasible, readily implementable

#### Business Impact Score (1-5)
**What they assessed**: What's the business value and potential?
- **1**: Minimal or negative business impact
- **2**: Limited business value
- **3**: Moderate business value, worth considering
- **4**: Strong business opportunity
- **5**: Significant business value, high ROI potential

### Score Interpretation

#### Average Score
- The score shown is the **average** from multiple reviewers
- Example: If 3 reviewers gave Innovation scores of 4, 5, and 4 → you see **4.3**
- You see the **aggregate only**, not individual reviewers' scores

#### Missing Scores
- A "—" (dash) means no scores were recorded for that dimension
- This can happen if reviewers didn't complete all dimensions
- Missing scores don't affect accepted/rejected decision

#### Using Scores for Feedback
- Low scores (1-2): Key areas for improvement before resubmission
- Mid scores (3): Meets baseline; consider enhancement opportunities
- High scores (4-5): Strengths to emphasize in future submissions

## What Comes Next After Decision?

### If Accepted ✅
- Your idea moves forward in the innovation pipeline
- Scores provided as **validation** of the panel's assessment
- Use scores to build confidence in your innovation ideas
- Share feedback with stakeholders or team

### If Rejected ❌
- Scores provided as **developmental feedback**
- Identify improvement areas before next submission
- Discuss with innovation manager for specific guidance
- Consider resubmitting improved version in next cycle

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why can't I see individual reviewer scores?**  
A: The system shows aggregated average scores to maintain reviewer anonymity and confidentiality. Reviewers can provide candid feedback without attribution.

**Q: What if I disagree with the scores?**  
A: Scores reflect the panel's assessment. If you believe there's been a misunderstanding:
1. Review the stage comments from each reviewer (visible in your dashboard)
2. Contact the innovation manager to discuss specific feedback
3. Request a clarification on particular dimensions

**Q: Can I resubmit and get new scores?**  
A: Yes. If your idea was rejected:
1. Address the feedback from stage comments and scores
2. Revise and resubmit in the next innovation cycle
3. New review panel will provide fresh scores

**Q: What if some dimensions show "—" (no score)?**  
A: Reviewers may not have scored all dimensions:
- Some dimensions might be less relevant for your idea type
- Reviewers may have focused on specific dimensions in their review stage
- Missing scores don't impact accept/reject decision

**Q: Who can see my scores?**  
A: Only you (as submitter) and the admin review panel. Scores are confidential.

**Q: Are scores permanent or can they change?**  
A: Scores are locked after Final Decision. They represent the panel's final assessment and do not change.

**Q: What's considered a "good" score?**  
A: This varies by context, but generally:
- **3-4**: Competitive and acceptable for most innovation programs
- **4+**: Strong candidates with clear business value
- **5**: Exceptional ideas at the top tier

## Using Feedback to Improve

### After Rejection
1. **Read stage comments** - Reviewers provide specific feedback on each stage
2. **Analyze low scores** - Which dimensions scored 1-2? Why?
3. **Identify themes** - Do multiple reviewers mention same concerns?
4. **Plan improvements**:
   - **Low Innovation score**: Add unique elements, differentiate from similar ideas
   - **Low Feasibility score**: Address technical/logistical barriers, explore partnerships
   - **Low Business Impact score**: Strengthen market analysis, quantify value proposition
5. **Resubmit** in next cycle with improvements

### Even After Acceptance
- Use scores to understand what resonated with the panel
- Maintain these strengths in implementation
- Address any "soft spots" (low scores) early in development
- Share results with your team for learning

## Technical Details (For Reference)

### Score Storage
- Scores stored when reviewers evaluate your idea at various stages
- Aggregated (averaged) when you view the summary
- Use simple arithmetic mean (no weighting)

### Score Visibility Timeline
1. **During Review** (Submitted → Final Decision): Scores NOT visible to you
2. **Final Decision Made** (Accept/Reject): Scores BECOME visible immediately
3. **Post-Decision**: Scores remain visible permanently

### Privacy & Security
- Submitter dashboard is private (login required)
- Scores visible only to you and admins
- Individual reviewer identities not disclosed
- No access to other submitters' scores
